# Audience personas (use these for your user journeys)

**1. Prospective learner: "Mercy", 22, Nairobi.** Wants a remote job. Uses a phone, limited data. Needs to see quickly what the programme is, whether she is eligible, and how to apply.

**2. Employer: "Maria", hiring manager at Savanna Digital Works.** Needs skilled freelancers fast. Wants to browse alumni by skill, see portfolio work, and contact someone without searching for emails.

**3. Donor: "David", programme officer at BrightPath Foundation.** Needs proof of impact: live numbers, locations, annual reports. Wants to subscribe to updates and get in touch.

**4. Alumni: "Fatou", digital marketer in Dakar.** Wants a profile that shows her work, a way to be contacted safely, and the alumni newsletter.

**5. The attacker (for the security team).** Fills forms with spam, tries to scrape emails and phone numbers, and guesses weak admin passwords.
