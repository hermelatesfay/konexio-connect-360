# Presentation outline (10 minutes + 5 minutes Q&A)

1. **The problem and the audience (1 min):** who is the site for, and what was missing?
2. **User journey demo (5 min):** a donor arrives and explores the impact; an employer finds an alumna and sends a message; the alumna receives the notification.
3. **Security and data (1 min):** what you protect and how.
4. **What you improved and why (1 min):** before and after.
5. **Challenges, lessons and next steps (2 min).**

**Checklist before you present:** links open; logins ready; screen recording backup saved; everyone has a speaking part; timer rehearsed twice.
