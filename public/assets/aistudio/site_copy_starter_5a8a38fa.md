## About
Konexio's freelance training programme offers a pathway from unemployment to integration into the online job market.
The Digi Pass programme follows European and international IT skills standards, is updated often in response to job
market demands, and uses real case scenarios from professional online clients. It puts a strong emphasis on soft skills.

**Mission (draft):** Equip learners with the digital, professional and AI skills they need to work online.
**Vision (draft):** A digital career for every motivated learner, wherever they start.
**Values (draft):** Empathy, Perspective, Openness, Curiosity, Humility (the EPOCH framework taught in the programme).

## Programmes
**Digi Pass: Part I (about 3 months).** Six modules, each lasting 2 to 3 weeks: orientation; advanced digital literacy
(Canva, Adobe, Google Workspace, Airtable); soft skills and advanced writing; understanding and implementing AI; introduction
to freelancing and BPO; workflow automation. Delivered in a hybrid model: in-person instruction, virtual sessions and
guided support through a WhatsApp chatbot. Learners earn the Konexio Certificate (Part I).

**Digi Pass: Part II (2 months).** Learners choose a freelancing pathway and work through intensive bootcamps, mostly
independent learning on external platforms (such as Coursera, Alison or Udemy), with small-group support from trainers
and webinars and mentor meetings with working professionals. Optional certifications depend on the pathway chosen.

**Pathways:** Digital Marketing & Social Media; Graphic Design; Customer Service & Virtual Assistance; Annotation & AI
Support; Translation & Localization; Data Entry & Market Research; Sales & Lead Generation; App & Software Assistance;
Cybersecurity Basics; Cloud Solutions; Mobile App Marketing.
